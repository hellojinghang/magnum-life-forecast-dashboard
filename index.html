                WHY THE DASHBOARD SHOWS TWO TYPES OF ACCURACY

                Timing matters more than a flashy score.

                V6 adds an economic requirement on top of all prior statistical gates. A model is not considered usable merely because it ranks winners better: its conservative expected payout must exceed the RM1 stake after time value, prize-sharing/cap assumptions and model uncertainty.
